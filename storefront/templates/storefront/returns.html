COMMON REASONS

When can I request a return?

Damaged item

The product arrived damaged or was damaged during delivery.

Wrong item

You received a different product from the one you ordered.

Product not as described

The product substantially differs from its marketplace description.

Product problem

The item does not work as expected or has another eligible problem.

Before sending an item back

  • Keep the original packaging where possible.
  • Include accessories and components that came with the item.
  • Do not send an item before your return request is approved.
  • Some products may have different return conditions.
  • Refund timing can depend on the payment method used.
