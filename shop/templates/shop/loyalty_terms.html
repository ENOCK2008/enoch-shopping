Loyalty Program Terms and Conditions

Welcome to Our Loyalty Program!

Thank you for choosing to be part of our loyalty program. Below are the terms and conditions that govern your participation.

1. Earning Points

You earn 1 loyalty point for every $1 spent on eligible purchases. Points are automatically credited to your account upon completion of your purchase.

2. Redeeming Points

Points can be redeemed for discounts on future purchases. Each point is worth $0.01. Minimum redemption is 100 points ($1).

3. Expiration of Points

Loyalty points expire 12 months after they are earned. Make sure to use your points before they expire!

4. Account Management

You can check your loyalty points balance and transaction history in your account settings. It’s your responsibility to keep your account details updated.

5. Program Changes

We reserve the right to modify or discontinue the loyalty program at any time. We will notify you of any changes via email or through your account.

6. General Conditions

This program is only available to individuals aged 18 and older. By participating, you agree to these terms and conditions.
