# Enock Shopping Center — Platform Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Marketplace foundation
- C2C + B2C seller accounts
- Catalog and categories
- Search
- Responsive storefront
- Authentication
- Cart and checkout
- Orders
- Reviews
- Seller profiles

## Phase 2 — Commerce operations
- Real payment providers
- Enock Wallet
- Seller payouts
- Shipping/courier integrations
- Tracking
- Returns and refunds
- Buyer/seller disputes
- Email/SMS/WhatsApp notifications

## Phase 3 — Internationalization
- Country onboarding
- Currency conversion service
- Tax/VAT engine
- Localized checkout
- Multi-language translations
- Customs/import workflows
- International shipping rates

## Phase 4 — Growth
- Seller analytics
- Advertising
- Coupons
- Loyalty and rewards
- Referrals
- Subscriptions
- Wholesale
- Business accounts
- Services marketplace

## Phase 5 — Intelligence & social commerce
- Enock AI shopping assistant
- Visual search
- Voice shopping
- Product recommendations
- Seller AI tools
- Messaging
- Live shopping
- Creator/affiliate stores

## Phase 6 — Apps and scale
- Android
- iOS/iPadOS
- Desktop clients
- CDN/object storage
- Background jobs
- Search engine
- Observability
- Multi-region infrastructure

External integrations must be configured with real provider accounts, credentials, legal agreements and country-specific compliance before production use.
